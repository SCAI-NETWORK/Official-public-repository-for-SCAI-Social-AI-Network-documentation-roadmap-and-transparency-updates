# Bot Suite Overview

SCAI begins with five autonomous bot engines.

## Token Creator

Token Creator is designed to help convert market narratives into launch-ready token concepts. It combines AI-assisted branding, trading parameters, launch readiness, wallet controls and post-launch monitoring.

## DEX Hunter

DEX Hunter monitors fast-moving decentralized exchange opportunities. It is designed around hot-pair detection, transaction pressure, liquidity filters, safety rules, AI sniping logic and real-time paper/live execution lanes.

## CEX New Listing

CEX New Listing focuses on official listing announcements, exchange readiness, AI review reports, launch calendars and controlled sniping profiles for upcoming listings.

## Whale Tracker

Whale Tracker follows major wallet activity, related-wallet movement, cluster behavior, exchange inflows/outflows, buying and selling pressure, and AI reasoning behind trade decisions.

## Arbitrage Bot

Arbitrage Bot monitors cross-exchange, triangular and funding-rate opportunities. It is designed to track edge, fees, slippage, liquidity, funding settlement, inventory balance and reconciliation.

## Design Principle

Each bot is separated so that one strategy cannot pollute another. Shared platform services such as notifications, security, worker supervision, exports and live-promotion gates remain centralized.
