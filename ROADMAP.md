# SCAI Roadmap

This roadmap summarizes the current public direction of SCAI Social AI Network. Timelines are forward-looking and may change due to technical, security, regulatory, infrastructure or market conditions.

## Phase 0 - Q1 2026

**Genesis And Infrastructure**

- Core project architecture.
- Seed investor round.
- Initial bot suite planning.
- Infrastructure design for private platform, investor access and smart-contract modules.

## Phase 1 - Q2 2026

**Sensing Engine Beta**

- Data-ingestion beta.
- SCAI AI data-learning layer.
- SolidProof audit preparation and review path.
- Platform and node optimization.
- New bot deployment: Double Bot.
- First reward event.

## Phase 2 - Q3-Q4 2026

**Expansion Layer**

- SCAI Essentials bot deployment.
- Five additional bot modules planned.
- New nodes and additional data-ingestion deployment.
- CertiK Skynet audit target.
- New SCAI Neural terminal.

## Phase 3 - Q4 2026-Q1 2027

**Global Node Expansion**

- Global node expansion.
- Beginning of SCAI AI algorithmic trading layer.
- Beginning of Token Creator Launchpad Suite+.
- Reward event.

## Phase 4 - Q1-Q2 2027

**AI Trading Maturity**

- Finalization target for SCAI AI Trading Algorithm.
- Token Creator Launchpad Suite+ expansion.
- Global investor access and feedback event.
- Final node deployment.
- AI trading deployment.

## Phase 5 - Q3 2027

**Final Upgrade And Public Market Preparation**

- Final platform upgrade.
- Stress testing.
- Presale close target.
- Listing announcement.
- CEX listing target.

## Roadmap Principle

SCAI is designed to prioritize security, platform reliability, transparent investor communication and controlled expansion over rushed public release.
